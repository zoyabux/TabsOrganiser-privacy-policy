# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 26, 2026

## TabsOrganiser Privacy Policy

TabsOrganiser respects your privacy.

### Information Collection

TabsOrganiser does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

### Local Storage

The extension stores saved tabs, folders, and preferences locally using Chrome's storage API.

No data is sent to external servers.

### Third Parties

TabsOrganiser does not share user data with third parties.

### Analytics

TabsOrganiser does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or cookies.
